Our Services

From diagnosis to treatment, we provide comprehensive primary care all under one roof.

🩺

General Consultation

Professional consultation and clinical examination for adults and children with any health concerns.

🤒

Diagnosis & Treatment

Accurate diagnosis of illnesses including malaria, typhoid, UTIs, respiratory infections, and more.

💊

Dispensing Medicines

Quality, genuine medications dispensed in-clinic. We stock a wide range of prescription and OTC drugs.

🩸

Laboratory Tests

On-site rapid diagnostic tests including malaria RDT, blood sugar, pregnancy tests, and urinalysis.

👶

Child Healthcare

Child health services including immunisation advice, growth monitoring, and treatment of childhood illnesses.

🩹

Wound Dressing

Professional cleaning and dressing of wounds, minor injuries, and post-operative care.

💉

Injections & IV Drips

Safe administration of intramuscular and intravenous medications prescribed by our clinical officer.

📋

Health Advice & Referrals

Personalised health education, preventive care advice, and timely referrals for complex conditions.
